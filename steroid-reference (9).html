Choose the site first — then prescribe with context

Topical corticosteroid selection should reflect body site, diagnosis, severity, lesion thickness, age, treatment area, and duration. Thin or occluded skin generally needs lower potency; thick plaques on scalp, palms, and soles often need stronger therapy.

This tool is designed to answer the practical question: “What could I reasonably prescribe here, and how should I write it?”
