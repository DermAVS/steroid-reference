About This Reference Tool

Topical corticosteroids are among the most commonly prescribed treatments for inflammatory skin conditions — including eczema, psoriasis, seborrheic dermatitis, and allergic contact dermatitis. They work by calming inflammation, itching, and redness, but the right strength depends heavily on where on the body they're applied: skin thickness and absorption vary widely from one region to another, so a potency that's safe on the sole of the foot could cause thinning and irritation on the eyelid.

This tool organizes topical steroids into seven potency classes — from Class I (super high potency) to Class VII (least potent) — and maps out which classes are appropriate for each body site. Selecting any region below will show you:

  • Steroid names appropriate for that body site
  • A step-down tapering schedule for reducing potency safely

A complete table of every steroid across all seven classes is also available at the bottom of the page.
